# Welcome Card — Text Templates

## For All 3 Properties · One Instagram Account

---

## CARD VERSION 1 — Phase 1 (Instagram Only, No QR Code Yet)

This is the card that goes in the property when Instagram is live but the email capture isn't built yet. Simple, warm, one job: get them to follow.

---

Front:

```
Welcome!

We hope you love it here.

Follow us on Instagram @thehandfulhomes
for availability, returning-guest rates,
and local tips.

Message us there if you need anything
during your stay.
```

Back:

```
WiFi: [PASSWORD]

Questions during your stay?
Message us on Instagram @thehandfulhomes
or contact your host directly.
```

---

## CARD VERSION 2 — Phase 2 (Instagram + WiFi-for-Email QR)

This version adds the QR code for email capture. The WiFi password moves behind the form — guests scan, enter email, see the password. The card still shows the Instagram handle directly.

---

Front:

```
Welcome!

For WiFi access:
1. Scan the QR code below
2. Enter your name and email
3. We'll show you the password

[QR CODE HERE]

Follow us on Instagram @thehandfulhomes
for availability and returning-guest rates.
```

Back:

```
Questions during your stay?
Message us on Instagram @thehandfulhomes
or contact your host directly.

Thank you for staying with us!
```

## Design Notes

- Print on cardstock, standard size (5x7 inches or A6)
- One card per property — the only difference is the WiFi password (Phase 1) or the QR code (Phase 2, if each property gets its own QR linking to a property-specific form)
- Keep it simple. No logo, no branding, no fancy graphics. Clean text, easy to read. The card is functional, not decorative.
- The Instagram handle is the same on every card — one account for all 3 properties.

## What the Card Does NOT Say

- "Book direct and save" — avoids Airbnb circumvention language
- "Book outside Airbnb" — same
- "Email us for future bookings" — too direct for the card, save this for the nurture email later
- Anything that explicitly steers guests away from Airbnb during an active reservation

The card says: welcome, here's WiFi, follow us, message us if you need anything. That's it. The direct booking conversation happens later — after they're following you, after they're on your email list, after their current Airbnb stay is over.
