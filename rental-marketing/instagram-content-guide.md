## The Posting Rhythm

**Goal: 2-3 posts per week.** Not more, not less. Consistency matters more than quality — a feed that posts every Wednesday and Saturday looks alive. A feed that posts 5 times one week and nothing for 3 weeks looks abandoned.

## What to Post — The 4 Types

Rotate through these. Don't overthink it.

### 1. Property Photos (the bread and butter)
- Clean rooms, natural light, honest
- Different angle each time — don't repost the same shot
- Morning or late afternoon light is best
- Caption: simple. "Quiet morning at [property name]." or "The view from the kitchen window." Don't write paragraphs.

### 2. Local Area (what guests actually engage with)
- Restaurants, cafes, walks, parks, anything nearby worth doing
- This is more interesting than your property photos to most people
- Caption: "5 minutes from [property name — best coffee in the neighbourhood."

### 3. Availability (the direct booking hook)
- "Sept 15-22 open at [property name]. DM to book direct."
- Post these when you have gaps in the calendar
- This is the post that drives revenue. Don't be shy about it, but don't make it every post.

### 4. Guest Moments (with consent)
- If a guest shares a photo or tells you they loved the space, ask if you can repost
- "Thanks for staying with us!" with their photo
- Skip if they say no or seem private

## The Weekly Routine (Pick a Pattern)

**Option A — Twice a week:**
- Wednesday: property photo or local area
- Saturday: availability or guest moment

**Option B — Three times a week:**
- Monday: property photo
- Thursday: local area tip
- Saturday: availability (if gaps) or another property photo

Either is fine. The point is: pick a pattern, stick to it for a month, then reassess.

## What NOT to Do

- Don't post 5 times in one day and then go quiet for 2 weeks
- Don't use heavy filters — the photos should look like what guests actually arrive to
- Don't write long captions. One line is enough. Two max.
- Don't post only availability posts — it reads as desperate, not inviting
- Don't stress about follower count. The only number that matters: are past guests following you?

## Content Ideas When You're Stuck

- A specific room from a new angle
- The kitchen counter with a coffee on it
- The view out a window
- A nearby restaurant's patio
- A park or trail within walking distance
- "Last week at [property]" — a回顾 photo
- Seasonal: "Fall colours from the back patio"
- A guest testimonial as a text post (simple, white background, their words)

## How Often to Check DMs

Once a day. Morning or evening, whenever fits. If someone asks about dates:
1. Check the calendar for that property
2. Reply: "Yes, [dates] are open! I can hold them for you. Payment by Interac e-Transfer to [email/address]."
3. Confirm once payment received.

That's the whole booking process. Don't complicate it.
