## Decision: Instagram, Not Website (For Now)

Instagram is the primary channel. No website yet. Reasons:
- A website is a project (design, hosting, booking integration, maintenance)
- Instagram is an account you post to — KW and LM can run it themselves
- 3 units with ~50 past guests doesn't justify website overhead yet
- Instagram DM IS the booking channel — guests message, KW/LM confirm dates, payment by Interac e-Transfer
- Website comes later when direct booking volume justifies it

## Decisions (answered Aug 27)

1. Instagram: ONE combined account for all 3 properties. Cross-promotion, less work, one audience. Split later if one property dominates.
2. WiFi: Each property has its own password. This means either 3 Google Forms (one per property, each with its own WiFi password in the confirmation) OR 1 form with a dropdown "Which property are you staying at?" that routes to the right password. The dropdown is simpler to manage — Mescalito decides.
3. Payments: Two accounts. One property goes to one account, the other two go to a different account. KW and LM need to tell guests which Interac e-Transfer address to use when confirming a direct booking.
4. Airbnb terms: Having an Instagram account and Google Form is fine. Do NOT explicitly solicit off-platform bookings on cards or in Airbnb messages. Keep language to "stay in touch" and "follow us." Direct booking conversations happen off-platform after the guest is in your system. KW/LM should verify in their host dashboard (Settings > Account > Terms of Service, look for "Circumvention" or "Booking Outside Airbnb").
