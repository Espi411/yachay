# Instagram Bio — @thehandfulhomes

## Options (pick one or mix and match)

---

**Option A — Simple and direct:**

Three cozy rental properties. Direct bookings welcome — DM us. 🏡

---

**Option B — Warm, a bit more personality:**

A handful of homes for your next getaway. 🏡 DM for availability & returning-guest rates.

---

**Option C — Functional, tells them what to do:**

3 rental properties. DM to book direct. 🏡 Returning guests get first access + better rates.

---

**Option D — Shortest:**

Three homes, one follow. DM for availability. 🏡

---

## Notes

- Instagram bio is limited to 150 characters. All options above fit.
- The 🏡 emoji is the only one I'd use. Don't overdo emoji in the bio — it reads as small-business, not professional.
- "DM to book direct" is the call to action. It tells guests what to do without saying "book outside Airbnb."
- The link in bio can go to the Google Form once Phase 2 is built. For now, no link needed — the bio is enough.
- If you want a location in the bio (helps with discovery), add the city or area name. Example: "Three cozy rentals in [city]. DM to book direct. 🏡"

## My Pick

Option B. It's warm, it's short, it has a personality without being cute, and "DM for availability & returning-guest rates" tells past guests exactly why they should follow — there's something in it for them.
