## Capture Method — WiFi-for-Email (NEW, primary mechanism)

The idea: guests get WiFi access in exchange for their email. This replaces relying on KW/LM making a manual ask every time.

How it could work (needs decision on approach):
1. Guest arrives → sees a welcome card or instruction sheet: "To get the WiFi password, scan this QR code and enter your email — we'll text it to you"
2. QR code → Google Form (name + email) → on submit, show the WiFi password (or auto-send it)
3. Email lands in the spreadsheet automatically — no one has to remember to ask
4. Also include the Instagram handle on the same card so they can follow

Advantages over the manual ask:
- Works for every guest, every time — doesn't depend on KW/LM remembering
- Fair value exchange — they want WiFi, you want their email
- Zero friction for KW/LM — the card does the work
- Captures the email at the START of the stay, not the end (no checkout rush)

Open questions to resolve:
- How to show the WiFi password after form submission (confirmation message? auto-reply email? redirect page?)
- Can a Google Form show a custom confirmation message with the password? (Yes — Forms settings allow a confirmation message, or a linked redirect)
- Security: the WiFi password is visible on the card's QR flow — is that acceptable? (Trade-off: convenience vs. someone scanning without filling the form. Can mitigate by putting password behind the form submit.)
