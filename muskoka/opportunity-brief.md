## The Opportunity

The District of Muskoka has a stated budget priority to "modernize service delivery and operations." They have a department — the Continuous Improvement Unit (CIU) — whose literal mandate is to "identify and implement opportunities for innovation and continuous improvement." They run legacy technology (iCreate CMS, PDF-based planning, manual meeting minutes). They serve 66K permanent residents and 2.1M annual visitors with seasonal capacity strain.

They're asking for what you do. You just have to show them.

## Why This Fits You

- You've built meeting-minutes-to-actions pipelines (RTR)
- You've built budget variance dashboards (governance toolkit)
- You've built document search with local LLMs (cyborg-infra)
- You understand municipal governance (RTR is a municipal accountability program)
- You have the local LLM infrastructure that means citizen data never leaves their network
- mB is a collaborator with municipal knowledge

The pitch isn't "buy an AI platform." The pitch is: "We can help your CIU deliver on the Strategic Plan's modernization goals. Here's a demo. It runs on your hardware. You own it."

Run it through the pipeline: audio → transcript → action items with owners, deadlines, and status. 2-hour meeting → 3-minute processing → structured output. Same architecture as RTR.

This is your competitive advantage. The pitch:

No cloud AI vendor can say this. This is the same value proposition as the elder companion (privacy-preserving, local inference) and the digital product ("Local LLM for Regulated Work"). Same IP, different market.

## Revenue Model

This is a service, not a product. Same model as the elder companion and the governance methodology:

- **Assessment + demo:** Free or low-cost ($2-5K) — understand their workflows, demonstrate one use case
- **Implementation:** $25-75K depending on scope — integrate the pipeline into their systems, train staff
- **Ongoing retainer:** $2-5K/month — calibration, updates, new workflows, troubleshooting
- **Expansion:** Lower-tier towns (Huntsville, Bracebridge, Gravenhurst) each need the same thing — 6 municipalities = 6 potential clients

One municipality at $50K implementation + $3K/month retainer = $86K year one. Six municipalities = $516K potential. This is a real business.
