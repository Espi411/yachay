## FORMAT NOTES FOR THE SCRIPT ITSELF

A 1-minute talk has roughly 4 beats (~15 seconds each):

1. HOOK (0-15s): State the thing that's non-obvious. Don't build up to it — lead with it. "Three books everyone references separately are actually one system."
2. THE INGREDIENTS (15-40s): Name each piece briefly. 5-8 seconds each. No deep explanation — just enough that someone who doesn't know them can follow.
3. THE LINKAGE (40-50s): The "aha." How they connect. This is the part that makes the talk worth doing.
4. THE ACTION (50-60s): One thing someone can do with this. Concrete, not abstract.

If you can't fit all four beats in 60 seconds, cut from the ingredients — they can be terser. The linkage and the action are the parts that matter.
